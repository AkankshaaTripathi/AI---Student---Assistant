About The Project

Hello! We are Jeet Pratap, Kaushal Singh, Khushal Gupta, and Jay Mishra — B.Tech Computer Science students passionate about Artificial Intelligence, Software Engineering, and Problem Solving.

Our curiosity inspired us to explore technologies including C, C++, Java, Python, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Flask. While developing this platform, we harnessed the power of Generative AI and integrated the Gemini API to build intelligent features for student productivity.

AI Student Assistant is our collaborative project created to help students study smarter through features like the AI Chatbot, Notes Summarizer, Quiz Generator, Email Writer, Study Planner, and Assignment Helper.
